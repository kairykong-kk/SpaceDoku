Multiple Chips appear in groups and are commonly used to trigger actions, filter content, or make selections. It helps users complete their current tasks more quickly and easily.
## Type
Chip has three types: Button Chip, Removable Chip, and Toggle Chip.
<div style="text-align: center"><img src="https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/fe535442c249471f9d0bc4c3fea4e21f~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image" width="1000px" /></div>

### Button Chip
Button Chip is similar to Button; both provide visual cues that prompt users to take action or make a selection. However, the Chip is used to enhance the user experience or encourage action, while the Button is used to move the interaction forward or take important actions. During use, avoid using Chip for important operations.

### Removable Chip
Removable Chip is commonly found in "Search History," as shown in the figure above. Users can choose to show or hide Removable Chip. Click the "X" at the end of Removable Chip to delete it.
### Toggle Chip
Compared to Button Chip, Toggle Chip also has a selected state. It is commonly used for filtering information lists and supports selecting multiple items at the same time.
