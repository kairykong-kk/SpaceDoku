Option presents preset options in a structured format to help users make decisions quickly. Each Option is an independent selection unit that represents a specific selectable item. Options may have mutually exclusive relationships (single selection) or parallel relationships (multiple selection) with other Options.

## Anatomy
To clearly convey its meaning, elements such as text, icons, auxiliary explanations, and other elements are typically used to help users understand the content of options and the differences between them.
![Image](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/dee9cbb7a84e4a50b6704e7ba4c98085~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)

1. Container: Height 48 dp, width adapts to content.
2. Text field: The core identifier of an option, usually a short keyword or phrase that directly conveys the meaning of the option. Be concise and clear to avoid affecting readability due to excessive length.
3. Icons: Adding icons enhances the recognizability of options, making them especially suitable for abstract concepts or scenarios where quick differentiation is required.
4. Checkbox: Including a checkbox emphasizes the selected state.

## Interactive behavior
Supports visual differentiation of selected, unselected, hovered, and clicked states, enabling users to clearly understand the result of their current action.
