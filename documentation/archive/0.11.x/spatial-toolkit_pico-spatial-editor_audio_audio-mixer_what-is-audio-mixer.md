In Audio Mixer, you can use audio mixing groups to manage the playback properties of audio files by grouping them. With Audio Mixer, you can:

* Control the playback properties of all audio files in an audio mixing group simultaneously, including mute, playback speed, and gain.
* Add audio mixing groups as audio components to entities.

## Use cases
The use cases of Audio Mixer are as follows:

* **Category-based volume control**: You can assign background music (BGM), sound effects (SFX), and voice to different audio mixing groups, enabling independent control of the volume for each type of audio.
* **Dynamic environmental sound effects**: When entering underwater environments or activating slow-motion and other special scenarios, you can adjust the playback speed of specific audio mixing groups to create dynamic auditory effects that match the scene.
* **Quick mute/unmute**: You can quickly mute or unmute an entire audio mixing group, thereby controlling the playback state of all audio files at once.
