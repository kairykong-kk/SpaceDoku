This article introduces the types of shaders supported by Spatial Editor.
## Built-in shaders
The built-in shader types in Spatial Editor include Physically Based, Unlit, and Cloth. The following image shows the visual effects of the Physically Based, Unlit, and Cloth shaders from left to right.
