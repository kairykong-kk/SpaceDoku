Currently, the main external devices are XR controllers. The controller is an important input device for PICO OS 6, offering efficient and accurate positional tracking as well as delicate and realistic haptic feedback.

* **Controller ray interactions**
   Controller ray interaction is a way of operating on a target by emitting a ray from the front of the controller. It is characterized by high efficiency and precision. Users can adjust the ray at the front end of the handle by rotating or moving the handle to aim at and select targets, and then drag them. When both hands are used simultaneously, actions such as scaling, rotating, and more can also be triggered.

* **Direct controller interaction**
   Similar to near-field direct interaction using gesture tracking, the controller also supports direct interaction by approaching the target. It likewise supports both Poke and Pinch methods for triggering a selection action on the target. Compared to gesture interaction, the controller provides more precise tracking and more responsive feedback.
   * **Poke**
      Trigger selection by touching interactive objects with the pointer at the front of the controller. This method is mainly used for UI panels, 3D buttons, and similar targets. Scrolling or dragging can also be performed on specific planes.
      ![Image](https://p9-arcosite.byteimg.com/tos-cn-i-goo7wpa0wc/30c64a886ebb4660805921aefc24517a~tplv-goo7wpa0wc-image.image)
   * **Pinch**
      Selection is triggered by pressing the controller trigger button and similar actions, and dragging is supported afterwards. Interacting with both hands simultaneously also enables zooming, rotating, and other operations.
