The presentation format of a video largely depends on the field of view (FOV). FOV refers to the angular range that a camera or display device can capture or display, and is usually expressed in degrees. With the development of shooting and display technologies, videos with different FOVs have gradually evolved their own distinctive features, including flat video, 180° panoramic video, and 360° panoramic video.
## **Flat video**
Flat video is the most traditional video format. It is shot from a fixed viewpoint, and viewers can only see the specific direction that the camera lens is facing. This format simulates the visual experience of the human eye in specific directions.
## Hemispherical panoramic videos
A hemispherical panoramic video covers a 180° FOV, typically referring to a hemispherical view of 180° horizontally or vertically. This format provides a more immersive experience than flat video while maintaining a relatively small file size.
## Panoramic video
Panoramic video provides complete 360° horizontal and vertical FOV, creating a fully immersive viewing experience. Viewers can freely rotate their perspective in any direction, as if they were actually present in the environment where the video was filmed.
## Comparisons
The differences among flat videos, hemispherical panoramic video, and panoramic video in terms of FOV range, characteristics, use cases, and other aspects are as follows.
| **Video type** | **FOV range** | **Main features** | **Use cases** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Flat video | Usually between 60° and 120° | * Passive viewing, cannot change perspective <br> * Flatness, lack of a sense of 3D <br> * Low production cost and low technical requirements <br> * Smaller file size | * Traditional film and television content <br> * Education and training videos <br> * News report <br> * Documentary <br> * Short videos for social media <br> * Live streaming |
| Hemispherical panoramic video | Accurate 180° coverage (horizontal or vertical) | * Partial immersive experiences <br> * Viewers can freely rotate their viewpoint within a 180° range. <br> * Medium production complexity <br> * Moderate file size | * Educational training, industrial training <br> * Virtual shopping <br> * Product display <br> * Display of sports events from specific angles <br> * Display of key areas of tourist attractions |
| Panoramic video | Complete 360° × 180° spherical FOV | * A fully immersive experience <br> * Fully autonomous viewpoint control <br> * High requirements for production techniques and equipment <br> * Large file size | * Virtual tours and remote exploration <br> * Concerts and live performance sites <br> * Reconstruction of the news reporting scene <br> * Art exhibition <br> * A first-person experience of extreme sports |
